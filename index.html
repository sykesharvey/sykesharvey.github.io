Harvey Sykes

I am a postdoc at Ruhr-Universität Bochum working under Gerhard Röhlre. Before this, I received my PhD at the University of Essex under the supervision of Alastair Litterick.

My research revolves around reductive groups and their generalisations. I am particularly interested in studying Kac-Moody groups. This often involves:

  • Tits buildings,
  • G-complete reducibility,
  • Ind-schemes.
For more details, see my cv.
